Public listings, paid access

Jobs are publicly listed for visibility, but full details remain behind the Tier 1 platform access to filter out unserious applicants and protect the quality of the recruitment process.

Verification-first hiring

Once a candidate is interested, the second stage focuses on document verification, passport and CV review, and careful application screening under admin and partner oversight.

Placement and visa support

Successful applicants move into the final stage where the team manages visa facilitation, travel arrangements, onboarding logistics, and deployment coordination.

Three-stage model

Tier 1

Platform access

Create an account, pay to access the platform, and view complete job descriptions before making a real application.

Tier 2

Application & verification

Upload CVs, passports, and supporting documents, then pay the job-specific verification fee as the application is reviewed by admin and partners.

Tier 3

Visa & placement

Approved applicants move into visa processing, recruitment placement, travel arrangements, and deployment support with a higher-level fee.
