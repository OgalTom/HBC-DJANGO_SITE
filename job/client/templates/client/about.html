About HBC Job Majuu

HBC Job Majuu is a registered and government-recognized recruitment and placement platform focused on connecting job seekers, employers, recruiters, and partner agencies across multiple sectors and international labour markets.

We do not limit ourselves to healthcare alone. Our recruitment model spans hospitality, logistics, agriculture, construction, engineering, transport, domestic work, skilled trades, and other labour categories where verified hiring and safe overseas placement are needed.

Our process is designed around trust, compliance, and operational accountability. Every recruitment journey is structured around transparent access stages: public job visibility, Tier 1 platform access, Tier 2 verification and application processing, and Tier 3 visa and placement support for successful candidates.

What the platform does

  • Publishes publicly visible jobs across many sectors while protecting the quality of the recruitment process.
  • Requires Tier 1 access for detailed job view and account onboarding.
  • Tracks each candidate through a structured ATS flow and document verification phase.
  • Lets admins assign applications to partner agencies and reviewers for screening and decision support.
  • Supports visa readiness, travel coordination, and final deployment for successful applicants.
